Lantern Reader

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Lantern Reader is used by children. It was built so that there is almost nothing to protect: no account, no server of ours, and nothing sent anywhere for us to see. This page explains exactly what the app does with information, including the one case where something does leave the device.

The short version
  • There is no sign-up, no account and no password.
  • We do not operate a server. Your child's learning record is stored only on your device.
  • We collect nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own — this is enforced by an automated test that fails the build if any appear.
  • One exception: if you let your child read aloud into the microphone, short audio clips are sent to Apple to be turned into text. Details below.

Who this policy is from

Lantern Reader is made by an independent developer. Contact details are at the bottom of this page. There is no company, no data-processing partner and no third party involved.

The microphone, and the one thing that leaves the device

Lantern Reader can listen while your child reads aloud so that it can follow along and notice which words she stumbles on. This is optional. If you decline the microphone, or turn it off later, the whole app still works — she taps instead of speaking, and nothing is lost.

Please read this part. When reading aloud is used on iPhone or iPad, the app uses Apple's speech recognition to turn speech into text. In the current version, that recognition is performed by Apple's servers, which means short snippets of your child's voice are sent to Apple to be transcribed.

This is Apple's own service, not ours. We never receive the audio, and we never receive a copy of anything Apple transcribes beyond the text shown on screen in that moment. Apple's handling of that audio is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Whatever happens with recognition, the following are true of this app at all times:

  • Audio is never saved to disk by Lantern Reader.
  • Audio is never attached to any error report or feedback.
  • The app keeps only the recognised text, in memory, for the sentence being read, and then discards it.
  • The microphone is used only while a reading exercise is on screen and your child has started it. It is not listening at other times.

We intend to move this to Apple's on-device recognition, so that audio does not leave the phone at all. This page will be updated when that ships, and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

There are none. Lantern Reader contains no advertising, no analytics service, no crash reporting service, no social media SDK and no tracking of any kind. It does not build a profile of your child for any purpose other than teaching her to read, and that profile never leaves your device.

Nothing in the app is a purchase, a subscription or a prompt to buy anything.

Children

This app is intended for children, used with a parent or carer. Because we operate no server and collect no information, we hold no personal data about any child — including none of the data that COPPA, the UK Age Appropriate Design Code or the GDPR would require us to account for. The learning record is yours, on your device, and you can delete it at any time (see below).

What you can do, in the app

Open Grown-ups in the top-right corner. From there you can:

  • See the full record of what the app has learned about your child's reading
  • Export a complete backup as a file you keep, and import it back
  • Reset all learning progress, keeping the profile
  • Delete the child's profile permanently

Deleting a profile removes it from the device. Because there is no cloud copy anywhere, deletion is final and complete. Deleting the app itself also removes everything it stored.

If you ask us for your data

We cannot send you your data, because we do not have it. The export function in the Grown-ups area gives you the complete record in a readable file — that is everything the app knows.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what happens to information, the date at the top of this page will change and the change will be described in the app's release notes. A change that introduced any new collection or transmission would be announced in the app before it took effect.
